Lotus TeaHouse Rules

Activity

Why it matters
A guild can only thrive when members participate. If everyone disappears for months without notice, it becomes difficult to organize events, build friendships, and support one another.

Guidelines

1. If you plan to be away for an extended period, let an officer know. 2. Real life always comes first. 3. Members inactive for long periods without notice may be removed to make room for active players.

Respect

Why it matters
Lotus TeaHouse welcomes many playstyles and personalities. A respectful atmosphere ensures everyone feels comfortable spending time together. Guidelines

1. Treat fellow members with respect. 2. Disagreements are normal; personal attacks are not. 3. Harassment, bullying, or discrimination are not acceptable.
