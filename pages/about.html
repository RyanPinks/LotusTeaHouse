At Lotus TeaHouse, our members come first.

Many guilds focus on power, rank, and leadership. We focus on supporting the people who make up our community. A strong leader matters, but a hundred members working together can accomplish far more than any one person.

We believe a mentor should guide and protect their community, not stand above it.

Why do we have three leaders?

A guild built around a single ruler becomes fragile. If one person leaves, everything can fall apart. With only two leaders, disagreements can become deadlocks. Too many leaders can make decisions slow and confusing.

Together they help guide Lotus TeaHouse while keeping the focus where it belongs: on our members.

Lotus TeaHouse is not built around a throne.

It is built around the people sitting at the table.
